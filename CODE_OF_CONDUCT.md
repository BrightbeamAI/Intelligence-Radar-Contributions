# Code of conduct

The Intelligence Radar is improved by people who disagree about evidence. This code keeps that disagreement useful.

## Expected

- Argue from evidence, and say where it is thin.
- Assume good faith, and address the point rather than the person.
- Declare any interest that could affect your judgement, and step back from reviews you cannot judge independently.
- Credit other people's work and sources.
- Keep private information private, including your own.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, insults or discrimination of any kind.
- Promoting a product or employer without declaring the interest.
- Pressure on reviewers outside the review, or attempts to buy or trade a placement.
- Publishing someone else's private information, or confidential material you are not entitled to share.
- Repeated contributions made in bad faith.

## What happens

The editors may edit or hide comments, decline contributions, and suspend or remove anyone who breaks this code. Report a concern privately to the editors through [Brightbeam's contact page](https://www.brightbeam.com/contact-us). Reports are handled in confidence, and nobody who reports in good faith is penalised.
